## 🧠 The One Big Idea

Use these 10 rules to **audit** any interface for usability problems — a shared vocabulary used across the industry.

---

## 📚 The 10 Heuristics

1. **Visibility of system status** — always show what's happening (loaders, progress).
2. **Match between system & real world** — use familiar language, not jargon.
3. **User control & freedom** — undo, redo, easy exits.
4. **Consistency & standards** — follow platform + internal conventions.
5. **Error prevention** — prevent mistakes before they happen (confirmations, constraints).
6. **Recognition rather than recall** — show options; don't make users remember.
7. **Flexibility & efficiency** — shortcuts for experts, simplicity for novices.
8. **Aesthetic & minimalist design** — remove the unnecessary.
9. **Help users recognize/recover from errors** — clear, plain-language error messages.
10. **Help & documentation** — searchable, task-focused help.

1. 10 heuristics = a usability **audit checklist**.
2. **Visibility of status** and **error recovery** are top wins.
3. **Recognition over recall** reduces mental load.
4. **Consistency** builds trust and speed.
