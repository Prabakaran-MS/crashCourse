# 🔄 The UX Design Process

> 💼 **Industry Perspective:** In professional frontend teams, **The UX Design Process** is applied to build reliable, scalable, and maintainable production software. Engineers are expected to understand its trade-offs, best practices, and how it fits into modern architecture, code reviews, and day-to-day team workflows.

> A repeatable process turns vague ideas into validated, usable products. Most teams follow a variant of **design thinking** / double diamond.

---

## 🧠 The One Big Idea

Good UX is a **process**, not a burst of inspiration: understand users, define problems, ideate, prototype, and test — iteratively.

---

## 💎 The Double Diamond

```text
Discover → Define  |  Develop → Deliver
 (explore)  (focus)   (explore)  (focus)
   Problem space      Solution space
```

1. **Discover** — research, gather insights (diverge).
2. **Define** — synthesize into a clear problem (converge).
3. **Develop** — ideate and prototype solutions (diverge).
4. **Deliver** — test, refine, ship (converge).

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Human-Centered Design

Keep real users at the center at every stage. Decisions are validated with evidence, not opinions ("HiPPO").

---

## 🔁 Iteration

Design is a loop: **build → measure → learn**. Ship, gather data, improve.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

1. UX is a **repeatable, iterative process**.
2. The **Double Diamond** alternates diverge/converge across problem + solution.
3. Practice **human-centered design** — validate with users.
4. Replace opinions with **evidence**.
5. **Iterate** continuously.
