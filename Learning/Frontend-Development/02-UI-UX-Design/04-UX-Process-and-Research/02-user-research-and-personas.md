# 🔬 User Research & Personas

> 💼 **Industry Perspective:** In professional frontend teams, **User Research & Personas** is applied to build reliable, scalable, and maintainable production software. Engineers are expected to understand its trade-offs, best practices, and how it fits into modern architecture, code reviews, and day-to-day team workflows.

> Research grounds design in reality. It tells you *who* your users are, *what* they need, and *why* they behave as they do.

## 🧠 The One Big Idea

Build for evidence, not assumptions. Research reduces the risk of building the wrong thing.

---

## 🧪 Research Types

| Type | Answers | Methods |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Qualitative** | Why / how | Interviews, usability tests, field studies |
| **Quantitative** | How many / how much | Surveys, analytics, A/B tests |
| **Generative** | What to build | Interviews, diary studies |
| **Evaluative** | Does it work | Usability testing, heuristic eval |

---

## 🎭 Personas

Archetypes representing user segments — keep the team aligned on who they serve.

```text
Persona: "Busy Priya"
- Goal: check order status quickly on mobile
- Frustration: cluttered dashboards
- Context: on the go, spotty connection
```

---

## 🗺️ Related Artifacts

- **User journey maps** — steps, emotions, pain points over time.
- **Empathy maps** — says / thinks / does / feels.
- **Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)** — "When ___, I want to ___, so I can ___."

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaways

1. Research replaces **assumptions with evidence**.
2. Use **qualitative** (why) + **quantitative** (how many) together.
3. **Personas** align teams on target users.
4. **Journey maps** reveal pain points across a flow.
5. **JTBD** focuses on user goals, not features.
